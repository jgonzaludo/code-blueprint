Plan Map in the Browser

For any editor, or no editor. One command starts a local page that follows your repo. Ask Claude for a feature anywhere and the plan lands on this map, then updates itself when the files change.

$ npx storyline plan storyline watching ./src (7 files) http://localhost:4711 storyline plan received: "refund support" 2 new files, 1 changed
localhost:4711/planwatching
You (in any editor, to Claude): add refund support. Refund a paid order, put its items back in stock, and write an audit entry.
