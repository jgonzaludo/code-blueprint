Plan Map in the Terminal

A full screen view for people who live in the terminal. The map is boxes you move between, Enter zooms into a file's exact lines, and p / a flip between the plan and the applied result. Click the keys below or press them.

storyline plan (npx storyline plan)

What you get

The whole project on one screen with the new files in their own band, then the exact lines of any file one keypress away. No browser, no mouse.

What you give up

Arrows between files are listed for the selected box instead of being drawn, and there is no free pan. Cell grids are good at boxes and bad at routing.

Keys

arrows move  enter zoom in  esc zoom out  b p a view  c check

Mock TUI. Files, blocks, line numbers, diff lines and arrows are computed from a real type-checked mini project. The check result for the handler arrow is staged.
